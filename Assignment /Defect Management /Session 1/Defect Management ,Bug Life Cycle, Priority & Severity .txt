4.
Write a short scenario where a defect in a UPI payment feature (like in PhonePe or Paytm) is found very late—after the app is live. Explain in 4-5 lines how the cost and impact of fixing this defect would be different if it was caught during development instead.

Answer:
UPI Payment Defect

Problem: Money is deducted, but the UPI payment fails.
After Release: Fixing the problem is costly and may affect many users.
Impact: Users may lose money or trust in the app.
During Development: The defect would be easier and cheaper to fix.
Conclusion: Finding bugs early saves time, money, and effort.
