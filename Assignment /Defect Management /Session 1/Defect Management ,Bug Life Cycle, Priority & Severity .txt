4.
Write a short scenario where a defect in a UPI payment feature (like in PhonePe or Paytm) is found very late—after the app is live. Explain in 4-5 lines how the cost and impact of fixing this defect would be different if it was caught during development instead.

Answer:

After the app was live, a UPI payment defect was found. Money was deducted from the user, but the receiver did not get the money. This caused problems for many users and needed more time and money to fix. If this defect was found during development, it would be easier and cheaper to fix before users were affected.
