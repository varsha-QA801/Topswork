Given these three bugs found in a movie ticket booking app: (1) Payment fails for UPI, (2) Movie poster image is blurry, (3) App crashes if you book more than 10 tickets at once — classify each bug as High/Medium/Low Severity and High/Medium/Low Priority, and justify your choices.

Answer:
Bug Classification

Bug	                        Severity	                     Priority                     	Reason
Payment fails for UPI	        High	                         High               	Users cannot complete payment.
Movie poster is blurry	      Low	                           Low	                It does not affect booking.
App crashes after booking     High	                         High                	App crashes and booking cannot continue.
10+ tickets

Simple rule:

High: Major problem or app crash
Medium: Affects some functionality
Low: Small visual or minor problem

Answer:
Bug Life Cycle

New → Assigned → In Progress → Fixed → Retest → Closed
                                      ↓
                                  Reopened
                                  
New: Bug is reported.
Assigned: Bug is given to developer.
In Progress: Developer is fixing it.
Fixed: Bug is fixed.
Retest: Tester checks it again.
Closed: Bug is working correctly.
Reopened: Bug is still there.
