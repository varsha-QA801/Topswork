Answer:
Sprint Planning – Flipkart Style Shopping Cart

1. Add to Cart
Task: Add a product to the cart.
Acceptance: The product appears in the cart with its price and quantity.

2. Update Quantity
Task: Change the product quantity.
Acceptance: The quantity and total price change correctly.

3. Remove Item
Task: Remove a product from the cart.
Acceptance: The product is removed and the total price is updated.
