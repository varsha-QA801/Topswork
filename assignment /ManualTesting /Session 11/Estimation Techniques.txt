Answer:
WBS – Add to Cart Feature

Feature: Add to Cart
Check button – Click the button and see if it works.

Check quantity – Increase or decrease the quantity.
Check price – Make sure the price is correct.
Check cart – Make sure the product appears in the cart.
Remove product – Check if the product can be removed.
Check unavailable product – Check what happens when a product is out of stock.
